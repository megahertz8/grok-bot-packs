# Room setup checklist (the instructor, in person, before the Company setup bot)

Not a skill and not installed by any bootstrap. The instructor follows it with the owner, on the owner's own account.

1. Buy the Team plan on the business's own account, with their card in their own checkout. Every person who will use Grok Bot gets a seat on the team, so the Team Rules and admin settings reach their bots.
2. Find Team Rules on the team's admin page. If present: paste each paragraph from the rules section of `../../shared/skills/kllei-tzevet.md` as its own rule, scoped to Grok Bot, with no heading or number, then check from a member account. If missing (a team bought through an X subscription, or a single seat): rely on the skill fallback, which every bot in these packs installs, and note the plan type.
3. Bot sharing: admins only. Public template sharing: off.
4. Connectors: allow only what this business needs, such as mail and the shared drive.
5. Bot email: on only if the owner wants bots to have their own address.
6. Extra usage: set a monthly cap or leave it off, as the owner decides.
7. Auto-review cannot be locked on a Team plan: confirm it is on for each person, and say aloud that it stays on.
8. Import the Company setup bot on the owner's account and continue with the owner.
